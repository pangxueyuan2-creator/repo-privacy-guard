# Code of conduct

We want participation in this project to be safe, respectful, and productive.

Participants must:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- welcome people regardless of background or experience;
- give technical criticism without personal attacks;
- protect private information and never repost exposed credentials;
- stop harmful behavior when asked by a maintainer.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disclosure of private data, and
abusive security testing are not acceptable. Report sensitive conduct concerns
through GitHub private vulnerability reporting when public discussion would
expose someone to additional risk.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove harmful content and may restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.
